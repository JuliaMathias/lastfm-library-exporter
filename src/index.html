How full-history exports work

Last.fm returns up to 200 scrobbles per request. Large exports fetch every page and may take several minutes. Keep this tab open while the export runs. The exporter retries temporary errors and will report a failed page before offering a download. Artist, album, and track counts are calculated from the scrobbles matching your filters.
